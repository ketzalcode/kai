  takes a lease; the gap is reported, never written into the item as `blocked`.
- The monolith stays authoritative until each pack's guarantees pass; move one
  department at a time, never 56 agents in one PR.
- `1.0.0` is reserved for the split; all groundwork ships on `0.x`.

**Drafted:** thin core (mission, vision, scope, seven non-negotiable
principles), four success measures, and four milestones — `partition-lock`,
